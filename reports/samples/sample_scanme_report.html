Control Area Detail

1. Firewalls & Boundary Protection

ADVISORY

Only the services you actually need should be reachable from the internet. Everything else should sit behind a firewall.

Open port 22/SSH MEDIUM ADVISORY

A remote 'control panel' for the server is reachable. It is encrypted, but if the password is weak, attackers can keep guessing until they get in and take over the machine.

Fix: Require SSH keys (disable password login), enforce fail2ban/rate-limiting, and restrict source IPs.

Email & Domain Security

Can criminals send phishing emails that look like they come from scanme.nmap.org? (Grade: F) ⚠ This domain can currently be spoofed in phishing emails.

SPF record missing HIGH

Anyone can send email pretending to be your domain.

Fix: Publish an SPF TXT record listing your legitimate mail senders.

DMARC record missing HIGH

Without DMARC, spoofed emails aren't rejected and you get no reports of abuse.

Fix: Publish a _dmarc TXT record, starting with p=none then moving to p=quarantine or p=reject.

No common DKIM selector found LOW

We couldn't find a DKIM signature on the usual selectors (it may use a custom one).

Fix: Confirm DKIM signing is enabled with your mail provider.

No MX record INFO

This domain does not receive email.

Fix: If it should receive mail, add MX records.
