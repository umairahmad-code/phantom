## Engagement process
1. **Scoping call (free)** — agree exactly what's in scope and the goal.
2. **Written authorisation** — signed engagement letter + scope (non-negotiable).
3. **Assessment** — testing within the agreed window, strictly in-scope only.
4. **Report & walkthrough** — deliverables + a call to explain findings.
5. **Retest (optional)** — verify fixes; issue the change report.

---

## Non-negotiables (protects you *and* the client)
- **No test without signed, written authorisation** covering the exact scope.
- **Stay in scope.** PHANTOM's scope gate enforces this technically; the contract
  enforces it legally.
- **Professional indemnity / cyber liability insurance** before commercial work.
- **Confidentiality (NDA)** on every engagement — you're handling sensitive data.
