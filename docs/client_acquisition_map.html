02

The four phases

Week 0–2

Phase 1 · Foundation

You can't legally or credibly charge until these exist. Do them once.

  • Pick one niche (e.g. law firms, e-commerce shops, dental/medical clinics, local SaaS). Narrow wins.
  • Business identity: name, professional email, one-page website, LinkedIn profile that says what you do.
  • Templates ready to sign: engagement letter, scope form, NDA (use a solicitor-reviewed template).
  • Get professional indemnity / cyber-liability insurance before any paid work.
  • Decide how you invoice and get paid; set your starting prices (see the sales sheet).
  • Sample report polished and exportable — ✅ already generated by PHANTOM.
Week 2–6

Phase 2 · Proof & credibility

Firms won't hand their network to an unknown. Manufacture trust.

  • Do 2–3 free or heavily discounted assessments for local nonprofits or friends' businesses — with signed authorisation.
  • Turn each into a testimonial + a short (anonymised) case study.
  • Start OSCP (or PNPT/CEH) — the certification clients recognise. Put "in progress" on your profile now.
  • Publish 1 post/week on LinkedIn: a sanitised finding, a plain-English security tip, a compliance explainer. This is your marketing.
Week 6–12

Phase 3 · First paying clients

Now hunt. Warm first, then cold, then partners.

  • Warm: tell everyone you know what you do; ask for one intro each. Local business groups, chambers of commerce, alumni networks.
  • Cold: work your niche list — 20–30 personalised messages/week (scripts below). Lead with their compliance/risk need.
  • Partners: web-dev agencies, MSPs and IT-support firms have clients but don't do security — offer white-label or a referral fee.
  • Compliance hook: target businesses that need Cyber Essentials / PCI for a contract or insurer — urgency sells.
Month 3+

Phase 4 · Repeatable pipeline

Stop hunting cold. Build a machine.

  • Referral system: ask every satisfied client for 2 introductions — every time.
  • Convert to retainers: turn one-off tests into monthly monitoring + retest revenue.
  • Raise prices as your track record and certs grow. Niche down further and own it.
  • Keep publishing. Inbound leads are the goal — they close far easier than cold ones.

The messages (steal these)

Short, specific, about them. Never attach a scary "I scanned you" — lead with their need and offer a conversation.

06

Red lines (protect your career)

Never do these — not even to win a client

  • Never scan or test anyone without signed, written authorisation covering the exact scope. Unauthorised testing is a crime in most countries.
  • Don't cold-pitch with "I scanned your site and found…" — it's often illegal and a terrible first impression. Use only public info before you're hired.
  • Don't take paid work before you have insurance and signed contracts in place.
  • Never exceed the agreed scope — PHANTOM's scope gate enforces it technically; the contract enforces it legally.
  • Protect client data — NDA every engagement, store findings securely, delete when done.
